EARTH OBSERVATION & OCEANOGRAPHY

Built on Marine Science & Earth Observation

Real sources, operational datasets, and peer-reviewed numerical models powering ORCA.

SOURCE 01

INCOIS Ocean State Forecasting (OSF)

WHAT IT PROVIDES:

Directional wave spectra, significant wave height (Hs), peak swell period (Tp), and ocean surface currents.

HOW ORCA USES IT:

Evaluates hydrodynamic boarding risk and establishes safe operational thresholds for coastal and offshore vessels.

REFERENCE:

INCOIS, Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES), Govt. of India (WaveWatch III & SWAN Numerical Ingestion).

SOURCE 02

PFZ Thermal & Chlorophyll Convergence

WHAT IT PROVIDES:

Copernicus Sentinel-3 OLCI Chlorophyll-a paired with MODIS/VIIRS Sea Surface Temperature (SST) fronts.

HOW ORCA USES IT:

Identifies oceanic frontal zones and upwelling dynamic boundaries for optimized, fuel-efficient pelagic fishing.

REFERENCE:

Solanki et al., INCOIS Multi-Sensor Potential Fishing Zone (PFZ) Methodology & Validation Protocol.

SOURCE 03

Copernicus ERA5 Ocean Climatology

WHAT IT PROVIDES:

30-day ocean reanalysis baselines, historical wave climatology, and Sea Surface Temperature anomaly deltas.

HOW ORCA USES IT:

Differentiates normal seasonal monsoon patterns from abnormal marine heatwaves and hydrodynamic hazards.

REFERENCE:

ECMWF / Copernicus Climate Change Service (ERA5 Global Marine Meteorological Reanalysis).

SOURCE 04

Authoritative Disaster Bulletins (GDACS & USGS)

WHAT IT PROVIDES:

Global Disaster Alert and Coordination System cyclone tracks, storm surge warnings, and seismic tsunami advisories.

HOW ORCA USES IT:

Strict authoritative ingestion ensures zero simulated tsunamis or false alarms, upholding maritime safety honesty.

REFERENCE:

UN/EC GDACS & UNESCO/IOC Indian Ocean Tsunami Warning and Mitigation System (IOTWMS).
